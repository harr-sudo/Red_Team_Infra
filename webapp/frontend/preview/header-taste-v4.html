Variant V4 — dials 10/10/7 — everything maxed except density. This is the statement-piece variant. Most expressive, most kinetic. The header is split into four zones with deliberately asymmetric weighting: a numbered leader chip (01), an editorial brand block with a 22px name and hand-drawn scribble underline, a dense chip rail, and a vertical "live ops" marquee on the right edge.

Motion is layered: the brand mark rotates and shimmers continuously, the numeral bobs, the operator dot fires a multi-ring pulse, the chip rail responds to mouse position via magnetic translate (event-driven CSS variables, no 60fps JS loop), and the combobox opens with a spring overshoot. Prefers-reduced-motion users see a calmer rendition with all loops and springs disabled.

The deployment selector keeps the same custom listbox combobox pattern as V1/V2/V3 — Tab to focus, Enter / Space / Down to open, Up / Down / Home / End to navigate, Enter to select, Esc to close, click-outside to dismiss.
